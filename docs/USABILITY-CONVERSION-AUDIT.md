# I Love Decants: usability and conversion audit

Branch `ux/usability-conversion-audit` (made from `main` at `5c4ca3b`). **Not merged into `main`.**
Audited with The Technical Team's usability and conversion checklist, on the whole storefront at 1440 px (desktop) and 375 px (phone). Pages: home, shop, product pop-up, bag, checkout, contact. The admin was reviewed in earlier rounds (see `CHANGES-perfume-parameters.md`).

Findings are ranked by expected impact: offer and form friction first, then proof and CTA, then speed, then polish.

## Fixed on this branch

| # | Checklist item | What was wrong | Why it costs sales | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Forms | **Contact form discarded every message.** It checked the fields, then said "We will reach out on the number you shared" and sent nothing anywhere. | Every enquiry (often a buyer asking which scent to pick) was lost, and the customer waited for a reply that never came. | The form now opens WhatsApp to the shop's number with the name, topic and message already typed; the customer just presses send. Phone became optional (WhatsApp shows it), so one less field. Button: "Send on WhatsApp". Fits the client rule that customers are directed to WhatsApp/Messenger. `ContactForm.tsx` |
| 2 | 5-second hero | The hero said "A little scent. A lot of feeling." but never said *what* is sold or *to whom*; the subhead was 26 words. | Visitors decide in ~5 seconds whether to stay. | Added an eyebrow above the headline: "Authentic perfume decants · Delivered across Bangladesh". Subhead cut to 18 words: "Try authentic perfumes in small sizes, 3 ml to 100 ml. Pay only for what you will actually wear." The brand headline is kept. `app/(shop)/page.tsx` |
| 3 | CTA copy | Main button "Explore the collection" stated no value. | Value-led CTAs convert better than generic ones. | "Shop decants from ৳230", using the live lowest price (it follows sales and admin prices). `components/home/FromPrice.tsx` |
| 4 | Trust by the CTA | Hero promises were vague ("Authentic scents", "Calm help, never pressure"). | Vague badges have about zero effect; specific facts reduce doubt. | Specific facts from the site's own settings: "Cash on delivery, no advance payment", "৳80 delivery inside Dhaka", "Poured from original bottles". |
| 5 | Proof where doubt peaks | The product pop-up had nothing reassuring next to "Add to cart". | That's where buyers hesitate. | One line under the price: "Cash on delivery · 1–2 day delivery inside Dhaka · Poured from original bottles". These are the same facts as the Delivery and How-we-work pages. `ProductModal.tsx` |
| 6 | Mobile sticky CTA | On phones, the price and Add button scrolled out of view while reading notes. | Mobile is most traffic and converts worst; a sticky action captures intent. | On phones, the price and **Add to cart** stay pinned to the bottom of the pop-up. Desktop is unchanged. `globals.css` (phone block) |
| 7 | Checkout friction | Name, phone and address had no autofill hints; the "Delivery charge" field had no linked label. The button said only "Confirm Order". | Autofill saves typing on phones; a risk-reversing CTA reduces last-second doubt. | `autoComplete` = name / tel / street-address; labelled field. The button now says **"Place order, pay on delivery"**. `CheckoutView.tsx` |
| 8 | Hidden bug | The checkout labelled "Inside Dhaka" by comparing with a hard-coded `80`. | A delivery price change in settings would mislabel every order. | Compares with the configured charge. |
| 9 | Reliability (empty shop) | A brief database error made the shop show "0 scents" with no retry. The developer's history has a "db timeout" fix, so this happens live too. | Customers see an empty store and leave. | Data requests retry twice on server errors (500 ms, then 1 s) before giving up. Sign-in errors are not retried. `lib/api/client.ts` |
| 10 | Button states | No pressed state; disabled buttons didn't look disabled (except while loading). | The site felt less responsive, and it wasn't clear when a button couldn't be used. | Pressed = slight shrink; disabled = faded with a "not allowed" cursor; loading keeps its own look. `globals.css` |
| 11 | Copy | 20+ em-dashes in customer-facing sentences. | Readers increasingly read these as machine-written, which lowers trust. | Rewritten with commas, colons or full stops; same meaning. Page titles keep their " — I Love Decants" separator. |
| 12 | Checkout progress (client request) | Nothing told shoppers how far through the order they were. | A visible "almost there" cue reduces drop-off at the last step. | A slim bar at the top of checkout: Bag ✓ → **Details** → Done, "50% · Almost there". On the confirmation page it fills to "100% · Order placed". Step names sit at their point on the bar (Bag at the start, Details at the middle, Done at the end), and the percentage sits under the end of the fill (client layout). Brand rose fill on the sand track; screen readers get the same as a progress value. `components/checkout/CheckoutProgress.tsx` |
| 13 | Phone input (client request) | Phone fields accepted up to 18 characters (spaces, dashes, +880). | Wrong formats were only caught on submit. | Every phone field (checkout, contact, sign-up, account) takes only digits, **max 11** (01XXXXXXXXX), with the numeric keypad on phones. A pasted "+880 1712-345678" becomes "01712345678". Helper `toLocalPhone` in `lib/validators.ts`. |
| 14 | Required fields (client request) | Nothing showed which checkout fields were required. | Shoppers find out only after an error. | A rose **\*** on Full name, Phone, Address and City, plus a "\* Required" note at the top of the form; `aria-required` for screen readers. Optional fields keep "(optional)". |
| 15 | Shop filters, desktop (client request) | To reach the lower filters (Size, Price) you had to scroll the whole product list. The panel was meant to stay in view but didn't, and it had no height limit. **Root cause:** `body { overflow-x: hidden }` silently disables `position: sticky` for everything on the page (this also affected the checkout summary and the admin's sticky header). | Shoppers who can't see a filter don't use it, and filtering is how they narrow down to a buy. | `overflow-x: clip` (still no sideways scrolling, and sticky works again). On screens wider than 900 px the panel stays 24 px from the top while you scroll, is never taller than the window, and scrolls inside itself; "Filter softly · Clear all" stays pinned at its top. Tested at 1366×768 and 1920×1080. `globals.css` |
| 16 | Shop filters, phone (found while testing #15) | After scrolling to the products and tapping **Filters**, the drawer opened 221 px above the screen and couldn't scroll. The drawer was as tall as the whole page (7,708 px). **Root cause:** the page fade-in kept a `transform` on the page wrapper after it finished, which makes every "fixed" element inside pin to the page instead of the screen. This was already in the original code. | Phone shoppers effectively couldn't filter. | The fade-in uses `animation-fill-mode: backwards` instead of `both`. It looks identical, but leaves no transform behind. The drawer now fills the screen and scrolls inside. Tested at 375×812. `globals.css` (`.route-view.route-active`) |
| 17 | Phone product pop-up (client report) | The pop-up looked stretched with an empty band above the photo, the × sat half outside the left edge, and the pinned "Add to cart" bar was cut off on the right. | Looked broken on the most-used screen. | One cause for the first two: a phone rule from item 10 (bigger icon tap areas) overrode the close button's positioning. The close button is pinned again, and the bar now matches the pop-up's 1rem padding. `globals.css` |
| 18 | Phone filter drawer (client report) | The × sat at the edge of its circle. | Looked broken. | Centred (a toggle-button rule had changed its layout). |
| 19 | Sizes (client) | The shop sells **up to 50 ml**, but 100 ml showed everywhere. | Promised a size that isn't sold. | Removed 100 ml from the starting defaults and demo prices (`lib/config.ts`, `lib/data/catalog.ts`) and from the engraving and returns copy. **On the live site the admin must delete the 100 ml size once:** Admin → Inventory → Sizes on sale → 100 ml → Delete. Tested locally: the shop then shows 3, 6, 9, 15, 50 ml everywhere. |
| 20 | Wide screens | On screens wider than ~1500 px the home headline broke onto 4 lines. | It pushed the buttons below the fold. | Headline size capped at its 1440 px value. |
| 21 | Sale price on cards | "From ৳270 ৳216" wrapped, with "From" on its own line. | Misaligned cards. | Price kept on one line. |
| 22 | Pop-up button (desktop) | "Add Quiet Bloom Eau de Parfum to cart" wrapped to 2 lines. | Messy, long button. | "Add to cart" on all screens; the full name stays in the button's accessible label. |
| 23 | Bag | The "added to your bag" message covered the item in the bag. | It hid exactly what the customer wanted to check. | No message when the bag opens (the bag itself confirms); oils and gift sets keep it. |
| 24 | Checkout errors | An empty "Place order" showed the error in a **green** (success) box, and the missing fields were out of view. | Misleading, and customers had to hunt for what to fix. | Error in red; the page scrolls to and focuses the first field that needs fixing. |
| 25 | Search | The header suggested 2 results for "vanilla" but the results page said 1 (they searched different fields). | Inconsistent and confusing. | One shared search rule (`searchText` in `lib/volumes.ts`); "From" capitalised like the cards. |
| 26 | Scrollbars | Thick grey Windows scrollbars with arrows in the filter panel and pop-up. | Clashed with the soft design. | Slim, tinted scrollbars. |
| 27 | **Photos for oils and gift sets** (client request) | Only perfumes could have photos. | Oils and gift sets sold on a generic drawing. | Inventory → Edit on an oil or gift set now has the same photo box as perfumes (up to 8, main photo, reorder, delete). Gift cards show the main photo instead of the drawn box; oil cards show it at the top. **New migration `20261125200000_item_photos`** (a photo belongs to a perfume or an item; nothing existing changes). |
| 28 | Admin wording | The Overview said "Details" and the Orders tab "View details" for the same button. | Inconsistent. | Both "View details". |
| 29 | Home hero arrows (client request) | The hero slideshow could only be changed with the small dots (or by waiting). | Shoppers browse faster with obvious next/previous controls. | **‹ ›** arrows on both sides of the bottle, symmetric (same 8 px inset, same height, 48 px touch size), on desktop and phone; they wrap around at the ends. Centred with `calc` so the shared icon-button hover lift can't move them. `HeroSwitcher.tsx`, `.hero-arrow` |
| 30 | Home page perfumes chosen in the admin (client request) | The hero showed a fixed list in the code (perfumes 1, 3, 5, 7), and "Find your easy reach" just took the first 6. | The client couldn't promote or remove products on the landing page. | New **Admin → Home page & content → Home page perfumes** panel (the tab was "Notice, categories & FAQ") with an on/off switch per perfume. It drives both the hero slideshow and "Find your easy reach" (up to 6). With none switched on, the home page shows all perfumes, so it is never empty; hidden or sold-out perfumes are left out automatically. **New migration `20261125220000_home_featured`** keeps today's four switched on. Tested: switching Soft Oud on and Sage Rain off changed the home page immediately. |
| 31 | Floating bag reminder (client request) | Once shoppers scrolled down, nothing reminded them there were items in the bag (the header bag scrolls away). | Forgotten bags are lost sales. | A round bag button centred just above the chat button, with the item count. It **only appears when the bag has items**, pops in and "bumps" whenever the count changes, and pulses a soft ring every few seconds (no motion for reduced-motion users). Clicking it opens the bag. It's hidden on checkout, and while the bag or the chat menu is open. Tested on desktop and phone. `components/layout/FloatingBag.tsx`, `.floating-bag` |
| 32 | Multiple photos per product entry (client request) | Perfumes could already take several photos at once, but the box didn't say so; adding a **perfume oil** skipped the photo step entirely; oil and gift cards showed only the first photo. | Shoppers judge decants by photos; one image (or none) undersells. | **Add product → Next** now opens the full form with the photo box for every shop product (perfume, oil, gift set). The box says "choose several at once or drag them in". Dragging several files onto it works, and choosing more than 8 stops at 8. Oil and gift cards in the shop use the same gallery as perfumes (main photo, thumbnails, zoom). Tested on desktop and phone: 3 photos at once on a new perfume, a 4th added later, 2 at once on a new oil; all reach the shop in order. `Panels.tsx`, `ProductEditor.tsx` (`PhotoManager`), `ProductGallery.tsx`, `ProductsView.tsx` |

**Admin walkthrough (real clicks, desktop and phone), all passing:** sign in; switch tabs; Add product → Next → full form with photo upload, every detail and prices → appears in the shop with photo and details; Edit → rename + new price → shop and Inventory row both update; Hide → gone from shop, Show → back; delete the 100 ml size; add a product sale (the shop shows it, and the bigger sale wins); add a discount code with an end date and usage limit; change an order's status and open its details; add a notice (it shows in the shop); open the manager form; upload photos to an oil and a gift set (they show in the shop). The admin edit window fits a 375 px phone with no sideways scrolling.

**Verified on a local production build:** home, shop, product pop-up (desktop and phone), contact (WhatsApp message built correctly), checkout. No sideways scrolling at 375 px. Typecheck and build pass.

## Found but not changed (need a decision, real data or bigger work)

| Checklist item | Finding | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Social proof | No reviews, ratings, customer counts or named testimonials anywhere. This is the biggest remaining gap. | Collect 5–10 real customer messages/reviews (with permission) and show one named testimonial near the hero, plus a real count ("1,200+ orders delivered") once it's true. A reviews feature is Level 3. **Never invent these.** |
| Guarantee | No visible promise beside the Add button (e.g. "Wrong or damaged? We replace it"). | The returns page already says damaged items are made right within 24 hours; the client could state it next to the button. |
| Sign-up form | Account creation needs a password (5 fields). | Guest checkout already works, which is the important part. Later: phone/OTP or email magic-link sign-in. |
| Type scale | 12–15 different font sizes per page (checklist: ≤6). | A design-system pass for the designer: map all sizes onto 6 steps. Not changed here, to keep the current look. |
| Product descriptions | Some seeded descriptions contain em-dashes (database content). | Edit in Admin → Inventory → Edit if wanted. |
| Speed | Home page: ~41 KB transferred, layout shift 0.037 (good, target < 0.1). | Upload compressed product photos (the admin already shrinks uploads). |
| Product pages | Products open only in a pop-up. | Own page per perfume (SEO, sharing, ads landing pages). Level 2. |
